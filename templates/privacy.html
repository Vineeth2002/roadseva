RoadSeva — Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy
Effective date: March 2026  ·  Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation
This privacy policy is published in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). RoadSeva is a civic grievance platform operated by Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC).

1. What information we collect

When you file a road grievance through RoadSeva, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • GPS coordinates of the reported location
  • Photo of the road damage
  • Ward or area name
  • Description of the damage (if provided)

We also collect your Internet Protocol (IP) address and browser type for security purposes only.

2. Why we collect this information

Your personal information is collected solely for the purpose of processing your road grievance and updating you on its status. Specifically:

  • Your name and phone number are used to identify your complaint and send you status updates
  • Your email address, if provided, is used for additional notifications only
  • GPS coordinates help field engineers locate the damage accurately
  • The photo helps engineers assess the severity of damage before visiting the site

3. How we use your photo

Your complaint photo is used to assess road damage severity and assign a field engineer. With your consent (given during submission), your photo may also be used to improve GVMC's road infrastructure services and to train AI models that help identify road damage faster.

If you do not consent, your photo is used only for processing your complaint and is not used for any other purpose. You can withdraw consent by contacting us at the helpline number below.

4. Who can see your information

Your personal details are accessible only to:

  • GVMC Grievance Officers assigned to your ward
  • GVMC Field Engineers assigned to inspect your complaint
  • The GVMC Commissioner (name and ward only, not phone or email)

Your personal details are never shown publicly. The public ward report pages show only complaint counts and resolution rates — no individual citizen information.

Your information is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party for commercial purposes.

5. Where your data is stored

All complaint data is stored on servers located within India. Data is not transferred to servers outside India. GVMC retains ownership of all complaint data — it belongs to GVMC, not to the technology platform operator.

6. How long we keep your data

Your complaint data is retained for a period of 3 years from the date of resolution or closure of your grievance. After this period, your personal information (name, phone, email) is anonymised. The complaint record itself (ward, damage type, resolution status) is retained permanently for civic planning purposes.

7. Your rights under DPDPA 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data GVMC holds about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to grievance processing requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for optional uses such as AI training
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact GVMC at the helpline number or address below.

8. Security

RoadSeva uses industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure session management, and photo metadata removal. Staff accounts are protected with strong password requirements and login lockout after failed attempts. All staff actions on complaints are logged with timestamps and cannot be altered.

9. Cookies

RoadSeva uses a single session cookie to keep you logged in if you are a GVMC staff member. This cookie is deleted when you log out or after 8 hours of inactivity. No tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies are used. Citizens filing complaints do not have any cookies set.

10. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights under DPDPA 2023:

  • Helpline: 1800-4250-0009 (Toll Free, 24×7)
  • Address: Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation, Commissioner's Office, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh — 530002
  • Portal: roadseva.onrender.com
