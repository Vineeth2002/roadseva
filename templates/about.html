Why RoadSeva Exists

The road complaint problem
every city faces

Across India, citizens report road damage daily — but reports disappear. No tracking. No assignment. No verified resolution. The result is public frustration, reputational risk for municipalities, and infrastructure that quietly deteriorates.

📱

No structured intake

Complaints arrive via social media, helpline calls, and WhatsApp — each in a different format, none with GPS coordinates or photo evidence, and none with a tracking mechanism for the citizen or the municipality.

🔍

No accountability chain

Once a complaint is received, there is no systematic way to assign it, track field inspection, verify on-site completion, or report resolution time to citizens or commissioners. Issues close on paper before they close on the ground.

📈

No executive visibility

Commissioners and senior officers have no reliable, real-time view of how many issues are pending, which wards are underperforming, or where critical road damage remains unaddressed.

🏘

No public transparency

Citizens have no way to verify whether their complaint was acted upon. Corporators have no data to show their ward's infrastructure performance. Public trust erodes.

What Municipalities Gain

Outcomes that matter to
commissioners and citizens

RoadSeva is designed around outcomes, not features. Every capability in the platform exists because a commissioner, an engineer, or a citizen needed it to work.

≤90s
Citizen Reporting Time
Any citizen can submit a road damage report from their phone, in Telugu or English, with location and photo — in under 90 seconds.
AI
Automatic Severity Classification
Every photo is assessed by computer vision. The system classifies damage severity and generates an engineering brief before a human reviews it.
GPS
Precise Site Navigation
Field engineers navigate directly to the reported site using coordinates captured at submission. No ambiguity. No wasted travel.
Live
Commissioner Dashboard
Executive view of citywide pending issues, ward performance rankings, and unresolved critical alerts — updated continuously.
100%
Audit Trail
Every action — assignment, inspection, update, resolution — is logged with timestamps and staff identity. Full accountability at every step.
Public
Ward Transparency Page
Each ward has a publicly visible performance page. Resolution rates, recent reports, and rankings — available to every citizen and corporator.
