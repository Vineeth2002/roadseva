Simple 4-Step Process
How to File a Grievance
📋
Step 01

You File

Fill the form with a photo and your location. Takes less than 2 minutes from your phone.

🏛️
Step 02

GVMC Receives

Your grievance reaches the concerned GVMC department instantly and automatically.

👷
Step 03

Team Acts

A field inspector is assigned, inspects the site, and raises a work order for repair.

✅
Step 04

Grievance Redressed

Once redressed, you receive a confirmation. Verify anytime using your grievance number.
