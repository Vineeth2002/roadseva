Road Quality Index

Tracks how long repairs last — surfaces wards and engineers where roads deteriorate fastest

🔍 How the RQI works
① Every time a complaint is resolved, the system records the GPS location, who repaired it, and the date.
② If a new complaint arrives within 50m of that location within {{ data.warranty_days }} days, it is automatically flagged as a warranty event.
③ The RQI score = repairs that lasted past warranty ÷ total tracked repairs × 100. Higher is better.
④ Use this data to ask questions about materials, methods, and workmanship — not to blame individuals.
